Powerful Features

Everything you need to communicate naturally in voice chat

💬

Emotion Control

Express yourself with 5 emotion presets or create custom emotional expressions. Convey feelings through tone, not just words.

🎤

Voice Design

Create unique voices from text descriptions. Describe a voice and bring it to life - perfect for characters and personas.

🎧

Voice Cloning

Clone any voice from just 3 seconds of audio. Preserve your identity or recreate familiar voices with remarkable accuracy.

🔊

Virtual Microphone

Route speech directly to Discord, Zoom, Teams, and any app. Works anywhere a microphone is accepted.

🔄

Microphone Passthrough

Mix your real microphone with TTS output. Speak naturally and use TTS together through a single virtual microphone.

⚡

Quick Speak

Save common phrases for instant playback. Perfect for gaming callouts, greetings, and frequent responses.

🚀

Live Streaming

Hear your speech begin within ~5 seconds — TTS streams as it's generated rather than waiting for the full clip. Real-time pacing for natural voice chat.

📣

Clear Comms

Play a preset audio cue with one tap — built for voice chat where synthesized speech can't pause or politely interject like the real thing. Call attention, signal you're coming through, or break an awkward silence while you compose your next message.

💾

Save Last Generation

Save your most recent take to a WAV file with one click. Build a sound library, share favorite clips with friends, or archive that perfect read.

🔥

GPU-Accelerated

Compiled inference + a persistent on-disk cache deliver near-instant warmups on RTX 30/40/50 series. The first launch primes the cache; every run after loads in seconds.

🔒

Fully Offline

Powered by embedded Qwen3-TTS. No internet required after setup. Private, secure, always available.

System Requirements

Operating System Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
Memory 16GB RAM (8GB min)
Storage 8GB free space
Audio Output device required

Required for Voice Chat: Install VB-Cable (free virtual audio driver) to route MyVoice to Discord, Zoom, and other apps.

The Installer (.exe) will prompt you to download VB-Cable automatically. The Portable (.zip) version does not include VB-Cable and requires a separate download.
