## Notes
  * 'tot. gap' is accumulated MID-UTTERANCE silence, derived as
    max(0, T_a/P - T_a - cushion). Total silence is conserved:
    latency saved at the front reappears as gaps later. The
    product trade is about WHERE the silence lands, and Clear
    Comms is an interjection tool, so the front is the worst
    place for it (memory/clear_comms_purpose_framing.md).
  * 'ratio' is segment 4 / talker segment. Story 20.1 SS2.7 used
    the same denominator, ((chunk_size + lookahead)/12)/P.
  * The short fixture is only ~2.3 s of audio; at chunk_size 10
    that is 3 chunks, so several rows never reach a release
    decision before is_final would fire. Those read 'never'.
