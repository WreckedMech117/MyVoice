## Notes
  * 'tot. gap' is accumulated MID-UTTERANCE silence, derived as
    max(0, T_a/P - T_a - cushion). Total silence is conserved:
    latency saved at the front reappears as gaps later. The
    product trade is about WHERE the silence lands, and Clear
    Comms is an interjection tool, so the front is the worst
    place for it (memory/clear_comms_purpose_framing.md).
  * 'ratio' is segment 4 / talker segment. Story 20.1 SS2.7 used
    the same denominator, ((chunk_size + lookahead)/12)/P.
  * At chunk_size = 25 a posted chunk carries 2.0 s of audio,
    which already equals the whole 2.0 s cushion budget. So on
    the shipped geometry the feasible and unreachable regimes
    release at the SAME point - chunk 2, the first push where a
    producer rate is measurable at all. The policy's effect
    there is entirely to stop the guardrail from binding.
  * The short fixture is only ~2.3 s of audio; at chunk_size 25
    that is a single chunk, so it never reaches a release
    decision at all and is carried by is_final. Those read
    'never' - which is the correct behaviour, not a gap.
